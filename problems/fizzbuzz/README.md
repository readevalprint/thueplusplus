---
title: FizzBuzz
---

Read one integer `n` from stdin and print the numbers from 1 through `n`, one per line. Multiples of 3 are `Fizz`. Multiples of 5 are `Buzz`. Multiples of both are `FizzBuzz`.

`n` is in `1..20`.
