# Leap year

Read one year and print `true` or `false` and a newline. A year is a leap year when it is divisible by 400, or divisible by 4 and not by 100. Year 0 follows that same rule.

The year is in `0..2400`. The Go test checks 1900, 2000, 2023, and 2024, plus seeds 4, 100, and 400.

Adapted from the Rosetta Code task [Leap year](https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Leap_year). That task is available under the GNU Free Documentation License 1.3.
