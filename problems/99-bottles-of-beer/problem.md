# 99 bottles of beer

Read one integer `n` from stdin and print the bottles song from `n` down to none. `n` is the number you would buy at the store. One bottle is singular. Zero is `no more bottles`, and the first of those two words is capitalized only in the last verse. Verses are separated by a blank line. The last line ends with a newline.

`n` is in `1..4`. The Go test checks 1, 2, 3, and seed 4.
