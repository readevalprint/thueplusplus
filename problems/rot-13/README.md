---
title: ROT13
---

Read one line and print that line with each letter rotated by 13 places. Other characters stay in place. The line ends with a newline.

The alphabet is letters, comma, period, exclamation mark, and space. The Go test checks `Hello, World!` and `a.b`, plus seeds 3 and 11.
